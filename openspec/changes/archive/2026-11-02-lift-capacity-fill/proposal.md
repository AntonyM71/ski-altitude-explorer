# Proposal

## Why

When a lift has no recorded capacity or ride time, the busyness model gives it a flat value for its type: every chairlift carries 2,000 an hour at 2.5 m/s. Decision 013 shows how wrong that is for the lifts that carry most skiers. On held-out data, a chairlift's ride time is out by 55% and a two-seater's capacity by 90%, and cable car capacity is out by 40%. Seats, names and length fix most of it. Half of all chairlifts in ski areas record their seats but not their capacity.

## What Changes

- **Seats** are taken from the data or, failing that, read from the lift's name (TSD6, Quad, 6er-SB, ペア...). Implausible seat counts are treated as missing.
- **Chairlifts:** a missing capacity comes from a seats × fast-name table, and a missing ride time from a seats × short-or-long table.
- **Gondolas**, including cable cars with 10 seats or fewer: a missing capacity comes from a seats table, and the speed stays flat.
- **Cable cars with 11 or more seats:** a missing capacity comes from a formula in cabin size and length.
- **Every other lift**, or any lift without seats, keeps today's flat value for its type.
- **The tables are frozen in `busyness/params.py`.** They're generated by the prototype script from lifts in ski areas, and don't move when the data is re-downloaded.
- **Each lift records where its capacity and speed came from:** recorded, seats, name or type. **BREAKING** (structure file format): this replaces the yes/no `cap_known`. The model version goes up, so every domain is rebuilt.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `busyness-lift-estimates`: how each lift's capacity and ride speed are found. It covers the order of evidence (recorded, seats, name, type), the plausible ranges, the lookups for each lift type, the cable car formula, and the source each lift records.

### Modified Capabilities

- `busyness-laps`: "Every lift runs at capacity" no longer replaces a missing capacity with a typical value for the type. The lift carries its recorded or estimated capacity (see `busyness-lift-estimates`).

## Impact

- **Code:**
  - `busyness/partition.py`: lift features get seats, length, estimated capacity and speed, and their sources.
  - `busyness/params.py`: the tables and the formula's constants, and `MODEL_VERSION`.
  - `busyness/network.py`: lift ride times use the estimated speed.
  - `busyness/structure.py`: the structure file and its fingerprint.
  - A new small module for reading lift names.
  - `prototypes/lift_capacity.py`: writes the tables for `params.py`.
- **Results:** every domain's structure file changes. Capacities and lap times shift most where chairlifts are old 2-seaters or new 6- and 8-seat detachables. The four test domains' fixtures are regenerated, and decision 011's comparison table is rechecked against them.
- **Engine:** unchanged. It reads capacity and route time as before and never used `cap_known`.
- **Docs:** decision 013's code links point to the built code (it stays Proposed, like the rest of the busyness model, until deployed), and decision 004's missing-capacity rule points to it.
- **No new dependencies.**
